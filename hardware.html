Getting started: Select a legally authorized 16,384-byte P1 program image that you already possess. The site does not provide, fetch, or transmit firmware. Then use the three buttons on screen, or the A/B/C and left/down/right keyboard keys.

Progress: CPU, LCD pixels, indicator states, and buzzer frequencies come from TamaLIB. Saves remain local and can be exported. Replaying extended time away may take noticeable processing time, and state restoration still has known limits.
